Help / FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I deposit?

Choose Deposit, select a payment method, transfer the amount, enter the transaction ID and submit your request.

2. When will my deposit be approved?

Your request must be reviewed and the payment verified before the balance can be updated.

3. Why is my deposit pending?

It means the request has been submitted but has not yet been reviewed.

4. How do I request a withdrawal?

Open Withdrawal, enter your payment details and submit your request.

5. How can I reset my password?

Use Forgot Password on the login screen or the password reset button in your profile.

6. How can I contact support?

Open Support, write your message and submit it.

7. Does submitting a request update my balance immediately?

No. A deposit request does not change your balance until it is verified and approved through the secure admin process.
